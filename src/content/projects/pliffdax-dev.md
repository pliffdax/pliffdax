A personal corner for projects, short notes and whatever I'm doing right now.

The front page is a desk: everything on it can be picked up and moved, and
the desk remembers where you left things. If that gets in the way, **tidy up**
puts it all back in order.

It's a static Astro site. The desk physics is a small hand-written spring
loop, and opening a card uses the View Transitions API.
