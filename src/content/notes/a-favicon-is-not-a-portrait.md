---
title: A favicon is not a portrait
date: 2026-11-16
summary: A 1024×1024 photo turns into a warm blur at 16 pixels.
---

My first favicon was the full portrait: 1024×1024 pixels, 2.4 MB.

In a browser tab it's drawn at 16 pixels, and at that size a face becomes
a light blob with a dark bottom edge. You can tell it's a person. You can't
tell it's me.

So the photo became `avatar.png`, and the favicon is a small copy of it for
now. A proper mark can come later.
